# Privacy Policy for OEDC Divine Wisdom

**Last Updated: October 22, 2026**

**OEDC Divine Wisdom** ("we," "our," or "us"), package name **`com.oedc.divinewisdom`**, operates the OEDC Divine Wisdom mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring complete transparency regarding how data is handled while you engage with the sacred scriptures, spiritual podcasts, and life wisdom solutions on our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains the nature of information handled by the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles

* **No Account Registration or Sign-In Required:**  
  You are never required to create an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers (such as your full name, email address, phone number, or social media accounts) to read scriptures, listen to podcasts, search life solutions, or track sadhana progress.
* **Offline-First & Local-Only Data Storage:**  
  All user interactions—including bookmarks, reading progress across the 700 Bhagavad Gita verses and 11 Mukhya Upanishads, personal notes, daily sadhana streaks, custom theme configurations, and cached audio preferences—are stored locally on your device (using private, sandboxed SQLite and Hive local databases). This data is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server owned or operated by us.
* **No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Personal Data:**  
  We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information or usage activity under any circumstances.
* **Sanctity of Sacred Contemplation:**  
  All core scripture reading and meditation features remain fully operational offline without any internet connection.

### 2. Third-Party Services & Automatic Data Handling

To provide sustainable access to authentic Sanatani wisdom, multilingual accessibility, and ongoing feature development, the App integrates standard, industry-leading third-party services:

#### A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
The App displays ethical, non-intrusive banner advertisements docked at the bottom of navigation screens. Sacred reading spaces (such as Upanishad and Gita contemplation views) remain protected from disruptive full-screen ads. AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous device metrics to serve compliant advertisements, including:
- Device Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
- IP address and approximate geographic location (country/city level)
- Interaction and diagnostic data (e.g., ad impressions, click events, frequency capping, and error diagnostics)

These metrics are utilized strictly for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and ad delivery. You can inspect, reset, or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:
**Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID**.

For more details on Google’s advertising practices, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

#### B. Translation & Language Services
To provide accurate descriptions and practical commentary in 12 canonical Indian languages (including Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, Odia, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, and Punjabi), the App queries translation services for localized descriptions when internet access is available, and caches the results locally on your device for immediate, zero-latency offline reuse. No personally identifiable user information is attached to translation queries.

### 3. Device Permissions & System Access

The App adheres to the principle of least privilege and does **not** request access to sensitive permissions such as contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, phone state, or exact GPS location. The permissions declared in the App manifest are:

* **`android.permission.INTERNET`:**  
  Required to load advertisements via Google AdMob and fetch optional dynamic multilingual translations.
* **`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:**  
  Used to determine whether the device is connected to the internet in order to gracefully switch between offline and online services.
* **Standard Audio & Text-to-Speech (TTS):**  
  Used locally on-device by the operating system's native TTS engine and procedural sound synthesizer to chant Sanskrit shlokas and play contemplative background soundscapes. No speech or audio recordings are captured or transmitted off-device.

### 6. User Data Control, Retention & Deletion

Because all personal progress, sadhana logs, and preferences are held exclusively in local on-device sandbox storage:
* **Absolute User Control:** You maintain total ownership and physical custody of your data.
* **Instant Deletion:** You can erase all data, reading history, and cached preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage via your Android settings:  
  **Android Settings > Apps > OEDC Divine Wisdom > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage**, or simply by uninstalling the App from your device.
