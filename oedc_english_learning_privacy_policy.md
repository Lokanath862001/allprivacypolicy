# Privacy Policy for OEDC English Learning

**Last Updated: October 23, 2026**

**OEDC English Learning** ("we," "our," or "us"), package name **`com.oedc.englishlearning`**, operates the OEDC English Learning mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency in how information is handled while you use our comprehensive English curriculum, grammar matrices, pronunciation labs, interactive games, and examination preparation modules.

This Privacy Policy explains the nature of information handled by the App, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles

* **No Account Registration or Sign-In Required:**  
  You are never required to register an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers (such as your full name, email address, phone number, or social media credentials) to access the curriculum, practice grammar rules, play learning games, or take practice exams.
* **Offline-First & Local-Only Data Storage:**  
  All learning data—including lesson completion status, CEFR level progress (A1 to C2), 12 Tenses mastery matrix scores, 3×3 dashboard heatmaps, interactive drill XP, daily streaks, quiz answers, and custom settings—is stored locally in private, sandboxed on-device databases (such as SQLite / Hive / SharedPreferences). This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server owned or operated by us.
* **No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Data:**  
  We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal information or learning activity under any circumstances.
* **Self-Contained Offline Utility:**  
  All primary educational features, grammar reference cards, tense formulas, past examination modules, and vocabulary drills remain fully functional offline without requiring an active internet connection.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We firmly believe in data minimization. The App does **not** collect:
* Personal identifiers (name, home address, email address, phone number, government ID).
* Biometric data or face data.
* Contacts, address books, or calendar events.
* Precise GPS location or geolocation tracks.
* Photos, media libraries, camera feed, or external storage files.
* Voice recordings: While the App utilizes Text-to-Speech (TTS) to synthesize English phonics and sentence audio, it does not record or upload your voice to any remote server.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Automatic Data Handling

To support sustainable maintenance, ongoing curriculum updates, and free educational access, the App integrates industry-standard third-party services:

#### A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
The App displays ethical, non-intrusive advertisements (such as banner ads and optional rewarded ads for bonus learning modules). AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous device information to deliver compliant, frequency-capped advertisements:
* Device Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
* IP address and coarse/approximate geographic location (country or city level)
* Basic performance and diagnostic data (e.g., ad impressions, click metrics, crash diagnostics)

These metrics are used exclusively for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and anonymous reporting. You can review, reset, or delete your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings:  
**Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID**.

For further details on how Google processes ad data, please visit:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

#### B. Google Play Services
Google Play Services provides core platform support such as app integrity verification, in-app update notifications, and basic anonymous performance reporting.

---

### 4. Device Permissions & System Access

The App requests only standard, non-sensitive system permissions required for operation:

* **`android.permission.INTERNET`:**  
  Enables the App to load advertisements via Google AdMob and check for optional curriculum updates.
* **`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:**  
  Allows the App to detect network connectivity status to seamlessly transition between online and offline modes without user interruption.
* **On-Device Text-to-Speech (TTS) Engine:**  
  Utilizes your device's built-in operating system speech synthesizer to play spoken English examples, phonics minimal pairs (e.g., short vs. long vowels), and sentence models. All speech processing occurs locally on-device.

### 5. Educational Content & Examination Prep Disclaimer

The learning material, grammar formulas, 10-year past paper analyses, and competitive exam questions (including UPSC, SSC, Banking, and state exams) provided within OEDC English Learning are developed strictly for educational preparation and self-study purposes. While we strive for absolute linguistic and syntactic accuracy, the App does not guarantee specific exam outcomes or scores.

### 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

OEDC English Learning is built for general audiences, students, and competitive exam aspirants. We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union.

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). If a parent or guardian becomes aware that personal information has somehow been provided to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove any such information.

---

### 7. User Data Control, Retention & Deletion

Because all personal progress, game scores, and custom preferences are stored locally in on-device sandboxed application storage:

* **Full User Ownership:** You have absolute control over your educational data at all times.
* **Instant Data Deletion:** You can permanently delete all local data, cached lessons, and learning history at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android settings:  
  **Android Settings > Apps > OEDC English Learning > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage**, or simply by uninstalling the application from your device.
