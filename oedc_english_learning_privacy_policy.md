## 1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles

* **No Account Registration or Sign-In Required:**  
  You are never required to register an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers (such as your full name, email address, phone number, or social media credentials) to access the curriculum, grammar matrices, pronunciation labs, interactive games, or competitive exam question banks.
* **Offline-First & Local-Only Data Storage:**  
  All learning data—including lesson completion status, CEFR level progress (A1 to C2), 12 Tenses mastery matrix scores, 3×3 dashboard heatmaps, interactive drill XP, daily streaks, quiz answers, and mistakes vault entries—is stored locally in private, sandboxed on-device storage (`SharedPreferences` / local SQLite). This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server owned or operated by us.
* **No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Data:**  
  We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal information or learning activity under any circumstances.
* **Self-Contained Offline Utility:**  
  All core pedagogical features, grammar engines, tense formulas, past examination modules, and vocabulary drills remain fully functional offline without requiring an active internet connection.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We firmly believe in data minimization. The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

## 3. Third-Party Services & Automatic Data Handling

To sustain high-quality educational software without mandatory paywalls, the App integrates industry-standard third-party services:

### A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
The App displays ethical, family-safe advertisements (such as adaptive bottom banners and optional rewarded ads for bonus learning milestones). AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous device information to deliver compliant, frequency-capped advertisements:
* Device Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
* IP address and coarse/approximate geographic location (country or city level)
* Basic performance and diagnostic data (e.g., ad impressions, click metrics, crash diagnostics)

These metrics are used exclusively for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and anonymous diagnostic reporting. You can review, reset, or delete your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings:  
**Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID**.

For further details on how Google processes ad data, please visit:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### B. Google Play Services
Google Play Services provides core platform support such as app integrity verification, in-app update notifications, and basic anonymous performance reporting.

## 4. Device Permissions & System Access

The App requests only standard, non-sensitive system permissions required for operation:

* **`android.permission.INTERNET`:**  
  Enables the App to load advertisements via Google AdMob and check for optional app updates.
* **`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:**  
  Allows the App to detect network connectivity status to seamlessly transition between online and offline modes without user interruption.
* **`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`:**  
  Standard permission declared by Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising identifier functionality on Android 13+.
* **On-Device Text-to-Speech (TTS) Engine:**  
  Utilizes your device's built-in operating system speech synthesizer to play spoken English examples, phonics minimal pairs, and sentence models. All speech processing occurs locally on-device.

## 7. User Data Control, Retention & Deletion

Because all personal progress, game scores, and custom preferences are stored locally in on-device sandboxed application storage:

* **Full User Ownership:** You have absolute control over your educational data at all times.
* **Instant In-App Progress Reset:** You can reset all XP, streak counters, and mistake logs at any time from within the app: **Settings > Data & Progress Management > Reset All Progress**.
* **Total Data Eradication:** You can permanently delete all local data, cached lessons, and learning history at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android settings:  
  **Android Settings > Apps > OEDC English Learning > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage**, or simply by uninstalling the application from your device.
