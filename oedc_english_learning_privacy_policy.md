## 1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles

* **No Account Registration or Sign-In Required:**  
  You are never required to register an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers (such as your full name, email address, phone number, or social media credentials) to access the curriculum, grammar matrices, pronunciation labs, interactive games, or competitive exam question banks.
* **100% Offline-First Architecture & Local Sandbox Storage:**  
  All educational progress—including earned XP points, daily streaks, unlocked topics, quiz scores, mistake vault entries, and audio preferences—is stored strictly on your local device using Android `SharedPreferences` and local SQLite storage. We do not transmit, sync, or store your lesson records on external proprietary servers.
* **No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Data:**  
  We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal information or learning activity under any circumstances.
* **Self-Contained Offline Utility:**  
  All primary educational features, grammar engines, tense formulas, past examination modules, and vocabulary drills remain fully functional offline without requiring an active internet connection.

## 2. Features & Pedagogical Capabilities

The App provides comprehensive, offline-first English learning features:
* **Authentic 10-Year Competitive Exam Hub:** Past solved questions (2014–2023) covering UPSC CSE (IAS), NDA, CDS, CAPF, SSC (CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS), Banking (IBPS PO, SBI PO, RBI Grade B), and Teaching/Eligibility (CTET, UGC NET) with Expected Value (EV) metrics and pedagogical distractor rationales explaining why right answers are correct and wrong distractors fail.
* **40-Level CEFR Master Curriculum (A1–C2):** Structured progression across Foundational (Levels 0–10), Intermediate (Levels 11–20), Advanced (Levels 21–30), and Mastery (Levels 31–40).
* **Scholarly Grammar Matrices:** Interactive formula breakdown for all 12 English tenses across affirmative, negative, and interrogative structures, plus Voice & Speech Transformation engines.
* **Mistakes Vault:** Automatic cataloging of missed practice questions for targeted remediation until 100% mastery is achieved.
* **Spoken English & Pronunciation:** Minimal pairs drills, scenario-based dialogues, and on-device Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio narration.

## 3. Tutorial & User Guidelines

* **Step 1 - Diagnostic & Basics:** Start with 20 foundational alphabet, phonics, and syntax units or jump directly into the 40-level curriculum based on existing knowledge.
* **Step 2 - Formula Matrices:** Explore the visual 12 Tenses matrix and Active/Passive voice algorithms.
* **Step 3 - Exam Practice:** Solve authentic 10-year past papers and review instant pedagogical explanations.
* **Step 4 - Vault Remediation:** Re-attempt tricky questions stored in the Mistakes Vault.
* **Step 5 - Audio & TTS:** Tap speaker icons to listen to native synthesized pronunciation offline.
* **Step 6 - Progress Management:** Review learning heatmaps, streaks, and XP points, or reset progress anytime.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Automatic Data Handling

To sustain free, high-quality educational access without mandatory paywalls, the App integrates compliant third-party SDKs:

### A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
The App displays ethical, family-safe advertisements (such as adaptive bottom banners and optional rewarded ads for bonus learning milestones). AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous device metrics to deliver compliant, frequency-capped advertisements:
* Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address, and coarse/approximate geographic location.
* Diagnostic & Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, click interactions, and fraud-detection logs.
* User Consent: In eligible jurisdictions (e.g., EEA/UK under GDPR), the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog is presented.

You can inspect, reset, or delete your Advertising ID at any time via:  
**Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID**.

## 6. Device Permissions & System Access

The App requests only standard, non-sensitive system permissions:
* **`android.permission.INTERNET`:** Enables the App to load family-safe AdMob ads, verify publisher cross-promotion updates, and retrieve Google Play Store listing metadata.
* **`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:** Detects network connectivity to transition seamlessly to offline mode when disconnected.
* **`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`:** Standard permission declared by Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising identifier functionality on Android 13+.
* **On-Device Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Utilizes your device's built-in operating system speech synthesizer. All speech processing occurs locally on-device.

## 8. User Data Control, Retention & Deletion

Because all learning metrics reside locally on your device:
* **Full User Ownership:** You have absolute control over your educational data at all times.
* **Instant In-App Progress Reset:** You can reset all XP, streak counters, and mistake logs at any time from within the app: **Settings > Data & Progress Management > Reset All Progress**.
* **Total Data Eradication:** You can permanently delete all local data, cached lessons, and learning history at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android settings:  
  **Android Settings > Apps > OEDC English Learning > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage**, or simply by uninstalling the application from your device.
