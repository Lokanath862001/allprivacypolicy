# Privacy Policy for Daily Inspire AI

**Last Updated: September 19, 2026**

**Daily Inspire AI** ("we," "our," or "us"), package name **`com.slv.daily_inspire_ai`**, operates the Daily Inspire AI mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide any personal credentials (such as your name, phone number, or email address) to use the App.
* **Local-First Data Storage:** Your favorites, custom quotes, typography settings, and wallpaper preferences are stored locally on your device using private local storage (`shared_preferences`). They are never uploaded to our servers.
* **No Sale of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information to third parties.

---

### 2. Information Collected Automatically & Third-Party Services
The App utilizes reputable third-party services to deliver ads, analytics, and content:

* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):**
  The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as your device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and general interaction metrics for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and serving relevant ads. 
  You can manage or reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**).
  For more information, please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

* **Unsplash & Web Image Search:**
  When searching for online backgrounds, search queries are sent over secure HTTPS directly to the image provider API to retrieve publicly available images. No personal identity or profile is associated with these search queries.

---

### 3. Device Permissions & Usage
To deliver features such as live wallpapers, auto-scheduled inspiration, and ambient screen effects, the App requests specific device permissions:

* **SET_WALLPAPER & SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS / BIND_WALLPAPER:**
  Used exclusively to apply wallpapers to your device's Home Screen and Lock Screen, and to power the interactive Android Live Wallpaper engine.
* **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & WAKE_LOCK:**
  Used to reschedule your daily auto-wallpaper and notification alarms when your device restarts, ensuring seamless daily inspiration delivery.
* **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:**
  (Android 13+) Used only to display scheduled daily inspirational quotes or reminders if you have enabled them in the settings.
* **SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display Over Other Apps) & FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Special Use):**
  Used exclusively for the optional **Ambient Screen Atmosphere** feature, rendering subtle visual particles/floating dust on your screen. A persistent notification is displayed while this service is active, and you can stop it at any time.
* **Photo / Media Picker (`image_picker`):**
  Allows you to select personal photos from your device gallery to use as custom wallpapers. Photos selected stay strictly on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

Uninstalling the App or clearing app data in your device settings will permanently remove all stored preferences and history.
