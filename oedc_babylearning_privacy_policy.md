# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 14, 2026**

OEDC Baby Learning ("we," "our," or "us") operates the OEDC Baby Learning mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. 

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

---

### 1. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Our App is designed for young children, including infants and toddlers. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union.

* **No Personal Information Collection:** We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal information (such as name, email address, physical address, phone number, or precise location) from children under the age of 13.
* **Child-Directed Treatment:** All advertising integrations within this app are configured with child-directed settings (e.g., Tag for Child-Directed Treatment - TFCD) to ensure that no interest-based or personalized advertising is served to users.

### 2. Information Collected Automatically (Third-Party Services)
The App may use third-party service providers that automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information to facilitate app functionalities and monetization:

* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** AdMob may collect device-specific information, such as the advertising ID, IP address, and basic usage statistics. These identifiers are used solely for contextual advertising, frequency capping, and ad reporting. No behavioral profiling or personalized tracking is conducted. You can review AdMob's privacy policy here: [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 3. Device Permissions
To provide a smooth learning experience, the App may request standard, non-sensitive permissions (such as internet access for loading ads or text-to-speech engine initialization). 
* We **do not** request access to sensitive features such as your device's camera, microphone, contacts, storage (photos/videos), or precise GPS location.

### 4. Data Security
We value your trust. Since our App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of personal data leakage or unauthorized access.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
