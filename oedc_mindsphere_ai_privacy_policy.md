# Privacy Policy for OEDC MindSphere AI

**Last Updated: October 2, 2026**

**OEDC MindSphere AI** ("we," "our," or "us"), package name **`com.oedc.mindsphereai`**, operates the OEDC MindSphere AI mobile application (the "App"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure user experience.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is processed, and your privacy rights.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles
* **No Account Registration Required:** You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide any personal identification (such as your legal name, email address, or phone number) to play and access cognitive training features.
* **Offline-First & Local Storage:** All game progression, cognitive training scores, brain points, difficulty calibration, challenge history, unlock states, and visual preferences are stored strictly on your local device using private offline storage (`SharedPreferences`). They are not transmitted to or stored on external application servers.
* **No Selling of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information or gameplay telemetry to third parties.

---

### 2. Information Handled Automatically & Third-Party Services
To support the development and free availability of the App, we integrate industry-standard third-party services:

* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):**
  The App serves advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous device information, including:
  - Your device's Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  - IP address and approximate general location (country/city level)
  - Diagnostic and interaction metrics (e.g., ad views, clicks, and frequency capping data)
  
  These data points are used exclusively for fraud detection, ad frequency capping, and delivering appropriate ads. You can view, reset, or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**).
  
  For further information regarding how Google processes advertising data, please review:
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  - [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 3. Device Permissions & System Access
The App operates under the principle of minimal privilege and does **not** request access to sensitive permissions such as contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location. The permissions used are:

* **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:**
  Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob and verify network connectivity status.
* **Standard Audio / Vibration:**
  Used solely for in-game sound effects, cognitive audio cues, and optional haptic feedback during challenge interactions. All audio settings can be muted at any time directly in the in-game Settings menu.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Our App is intended for general audiences interested in cognitive skills, logic puzzles, and brain training. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt corrective action.

---

### 5. Data Security, Control & Deletion
Because your game progress and preferences remain strictly stored on your own device:
- **Full User Control:** You maintain absolute control over your data.
- **Instant Deletion:** Clearing the App’s data/cache in your Android system settings (**Settings > Apps > OEDC MindSphere AI > Storage > Clear Data**) or uninstalling the App will permanently delete all stored game progress, profiles, and settings from your device.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published in this repository with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
